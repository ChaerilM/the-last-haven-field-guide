## Ratios

The often-repeated **3 greenhouses : 1 kitchen** ratio should be treated as a
starting hypothesis, not a hard rule, until current-version throughput is
measured.

A useful test is to record, over a fixed in-game period:

1. Greenhouse food output
2. Kitchen food input/processing
3. Worker travel time
4. Population served
5. Temperature
6. Electricity state

That will allow a reliable current-version greenhouse-to-kitchen ratio.
