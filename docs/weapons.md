## Notes

Ammo type is a compatibility category, not a simple weapon tier. Compare
damage, range, accuracy, rate of fire, magazine size, production cost, and
availability separately.

Where only production cost/time has been verified, do not infer the rest of
the weapon's stats from old patch notes.
