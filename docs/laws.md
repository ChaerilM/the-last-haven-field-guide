## Stability strategy

**Steady hand** is a promising late-game Stability law because it boosts
Stability *gain* by 30%. The tooltip does **not** say that it creates a passive
Stability gain or immediately adds 30 Stability points. A separate source of
positive Stability is therefore still needed.

In the Dictatorship tree, **Steady hand** is below earlier laws. The
prerequisites and their costs must be considered before estimating the total
cost to reach it.

**Carrot and stick** modifies the chance of prisoner death; the tooltip
does not claim a direct Stability benefit.
